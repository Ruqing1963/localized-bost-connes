\section*{The chapters and the papers}
The book was assembled from the foundational paper and Papers I--XXV and XXVII--XXXIX of
the series, thirty-nine papers in total; the numeral XXVI is retired.
Each chapter is one paper, unchanged in its mathematics; the table gives the
correspondence and the directory of the source in the repository.

\section*{Numbering and cross-references}
Within each chapter, theorems, propositions, lemmas, corollaries, definitions,
remarks and examples share one counter and are numbered \emph{chapter.section.item};
equations likewise. These are the numbers of the original papers with the chapter
number prefixed, so a citation of the form ``[Ch.~4, Thm.~3.6]'' refers to
Theorem~4.3.6 of this book and to Theorem~3.6 of Paper~III. Citations between
papers have been converted mechanically to this form; references to the
published literature are collected in the single bibliography at the end.

\section*{What has not been harmonised}
\begin{itemize}
\item Prose references of the form ``Paper~N'' have been converted mechanically to
      chapter references, and the standalone-paper boilerplate of each first section
      (the ``series'' paragraphs) has been removed at assembly; occasional phrasings that
      read paper-wise rather than book-wise may remain.
\item Each chapter restates the notation it needs in its first section; the
      restatements are consistent (the same macros are used throughout) but repetitive,
      and could be replaced by references to the notation page.
\item The ``Assessment'' section that closes each chapter records what was and was not
      established at the time of writing. Some of these are answered by later chapters
      (Chapter~\ref{ch:XVIII} withdraws a suggestion of Chapter~\ref{ch:XVII};
      Chapter~\ref{ch:XX} answers Chapter~\ref{ch:XIX}); the answers are cross-referenced
      in the closing chapter on open problems but the assessments themselves have not
      been rewritten.
\item The twelve working notes in \texttt{paper/} that preceded Chapters~\ref{ch:I}--\ref{ch:V}
      are superseded and are not part of the book.
\end{itemize}

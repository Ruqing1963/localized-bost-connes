\chapter*{Standing notation}
\markboth{Standing notation}{Standing notation}

The following symbols are used throughout with the meaning fixed in
Chapter~\ref{ch:Main}; chapters that extend them (to function fields, to knots, to
non-commutative semigroups) say so at the point of extension.

\begin{center}\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{lp{9.2cm}}
\hline
$K$, $P_K$, $S$ & a number field, its set of finite primes, and an arbitrary subset $S\subseteq P_K$\\
$\Ns\pp$ & the absolute norm of a prime $\pp$; for $K=\Q$ rational primes are written $\ell$\\
$I_S$, $J_S$ & the group of fractional ideals, and the monoid of integral ideals, supported on $S$\\
$\cA_{K,S}=C(Y_S)\rtimes J_S$ & the $S$-local Bost--Connes system, with its dynamics $\sigma_t$\\
$\zeta_{K,S}(\beta)=\prod_{\pp\in S}(1-\Ns\pp^{-\beta})^{-1}$ & the partition function\\
$\beta_c(S)$ & the convergence exponent of $\sum_{\pp\in S}\Ns\pp^{-\beta}$\\
$G_S=\Idl_S/\overline{K^\times_{S,+}}$ & the $S$-local symmetry group; for $S=P_K$, $G_S\cong\Gal(K^{ab}/K)$\\
$1\to U_S/\overline{\cO^\times_{K,+}}\to G_S\to\Cl^+_S\to1$ & the exact sequence of $G_S$, with $\Cl^+_S$ the subgroup of the narrow class group generated by $S$, and $h^+_S=|\Cl^+_S|$\\
$\sigma:I_S\to G_S$, $\sigma_\pp$, $\Frob_\pp$ & the canonical homomorphism; $\chi(\sigma_\pp)=\overline{\chi(\Frob_\pp)}$ (Artin map normalised arithmetically)\\
$\Xi_\beta(K,S)$ & the obstruction group $\{\chi\in\widehat{G_S}:\sum_{\pp\in S}\Ns\pp^{-\beta}|1-\chi(\sigma_\pp)|^2<\infty\}$\\
$\mathrm{KMS}_\beta(\cA_{K,S})\cong\Prob(G_S/\Xi_\beta^\perp)$ & the classification (Chapter~\ref{ch:Main}, Theorem~\ref{Main:thm:main})\\
$\Psi$ & the pointwise invariant separating extremal states in the density-versus-measure example\\
$M_{K,S,\beta}$ & the von Neumann algebra generated by an extremal $\mathrm{KMS}_\beta$ state\\
$\Sigma_\beta$ & the type invariant of $M_{K,S,\beta}$ (Chapter~\ref{ch:I})\\
$\partial\cA$, $\cA^{\mathrm{aff}}$ & the boundary quotient and the affine (additive) control system (Chapter~\ref{ch:II})\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\medskip\noindent
Conventions: the Artin map is normalised arithmetically, so that a uniformiser at $\pp$
maps to the arithmetic Frobenius; the \emph{narrow} class group is not a convention but is
forced by $\A_{K,f}^\times/\overline{K^\times_+}\cong\Gal(K^{ab}/K)$; sets of primes are
sets of finite primes; all $S$ are countable, so the measure spaces are standard Borel.
